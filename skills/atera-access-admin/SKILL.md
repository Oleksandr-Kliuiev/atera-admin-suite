# Atera Access Admin

Resolve technicians by exact email and stable user identifier. Determine account mode and verify whether role scope applies to Customers or Sites. Treat visibility, permission, organization scope, and excluded folders as separate dimensions.

Read [references/access-operations.md](references/access-operations.md) for preset roles, least privilege, group membership, scope limits, deactivation, pagination, and verification.

New technicians may begin with the preset view-only Beginner role. The preset Admin role is unrestricted and at least one Admin must remain. Never assign Admin, script execution, patch/software management, remote access, registry, payment/billing, role administration, or all-organization scope merely because a peer has it.

Before deactivation or privilege reduction, identify ticket ownership, technician groups, automation references, private queues, customer/site responsibility, API/integration ownership, and remaining Admin coverage. Reassign required ownership first.

Finish with exact technician, role and scope before/after, group membership, excluded folders, privileged capabilities, reassignments, invitation/login state, and human authentication steps.
