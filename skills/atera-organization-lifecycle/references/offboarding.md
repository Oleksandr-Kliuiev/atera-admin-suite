# Customer or Site offboarding

## Preflight

Verify exact organization ID, requested end date, retention/export requirements, device ownership, agent-removal intent, active contracts, uninvoiced work, open tickets, alerts, scheduled automations, integrations, contacts/users, and responsible technicians.

## Safe workflow

1. Capture organization, device, ticket, contract, profile, integration, and billing state.
2. Export only the authorized operational and billing evidence to a private location.
3. Disable future ticket intake, schedules, or integrations only when explicitly requested and after checking shared dependencies.
4. Resolve or transfer open tickets, alerts, contacts/users, technician responsibilities, and monitored-device dependencies.
5. Remove assignments that would continue patching, scripts, software, billing, or notifications.
6. Handle every agent through the device-lifecycle rules; organization offboarding does not imply agent deletion.
7. End contracts and billing with exact dates and review unbilled work.
8. Delete the Customer/Site only when explicitly authorized and the UI confirms dependency clearance.

Preserve required ticket, audit, and billing history. Report retained records, removed access, stopped schedules, remaining agents, exports, final billing state, and irreversible actions.
